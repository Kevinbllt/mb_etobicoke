Methodology

Microblading vs nano brows vs powder brows

The right technique depends on your skin, not your taste.

Microblading

A handheld blade cuts fine strokes that read as individual hairs. Best on normal to dry skin. On oily skin the strokes blur and fade noticeably faster.

Nano brows

A machine with a single needle lays down the same hair strokes with far less trauma. Holds up on oily and sensitive skin, and heals more predictably.

Powder brows

Sold as ombré brows. Thousands of tiny dots build a soft gradient, like brow powder that stays put. Suits every skin type and outlasts the other two.

Before you book

What separates a good brow artist from a cheap one.

Semi-permanent still means a year or more of living with the result. An hour of due diligence is cheap by comparison.

Five things to check

  • Healed photos at six to eight weeks, not fresh work, because fresh brows always look good.
  • A current Toronto Public Health inspection for personal service settings.
  • A patch test if your skin is reactive or you have a history of pigment allergy.
  • A mapping consultation before any pigment. If they skip it, leave.
  • A written correction policy for when you dislike the shape.

The first ten days

  • Keep it dry. No swimming, sauna, steam or hard training.
  • Let the flaking fall off on its own, because picking pulls pigment out with it.
  • Around week two the colour looks like it vanished. It comes back.
  • Once healed: sunscreen, and no retinol or acids near the brow.

Questions

How much does microblading cost in Etobicoke?

Expect $450 to $850 for the first session, which normally includes the perfecting visit six to eight weeks later. Powder and combination brows sit at the upper end. Annual colour boosts are $150 to $300. Prices are quoted at consultation rather than published.

How long does microblading last?

One to three years, fading rather than changing colour. Oily skin sits at the short end. Most people book a colour boost every twelve to eighteen months to keep the shape crisp.

Does microblading hurt?

Most clients describe pressure and scratching rather than pain. Topical numbing cream goes on before the session and is reapplied partway through. The brow area is considerably less sensitive than the lip.

Who should not get microblading?

Pregnancy and breastfeeding, blood thinners, Accutane within six months, uncontrolled diabetes, a history of keloid scarring, and active breakouts or eczema in the brow area are all reasons to wait or skip. Botox needs two to four weeks of clearance. Ask your doctor as well as your artist.

Can microblading be removed?

Yes. Saline removal is the usual route for semi-permanent pigment and takes two to five sessions spaced six weeks apart. Laser works but can darken certain pigments. Within forty-eight hours of the appointment, emergency saline removal is far easier.
